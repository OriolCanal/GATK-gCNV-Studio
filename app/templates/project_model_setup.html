DetermineGermlineContigPloidy requires at least dos samples.
Interval filteringFilterIntervals is optional

1. Low-count filter

Elimina regions with very pocos reads in to substantial proportion of samples. Usa the counts per interval of CollectReadCounts; are números of reads, not depth mean per base.

Ejemplo: with threshold 10 and 100 samples, if 60 samples tienen fewer than 10 reads and the percentage is set to 50 %, GATK excludes the interval for the cohort. Si only 40 samples tienen fewer than 10 reads, retains it. Una sample aislada with low count is not sufficient.

Utilidad: retirar regions that presentan coverage issues of forma systematic, for example due to poor capture or low mappability.

2. Extreme-count filter

Elimina regions with to exceptionally low or high coverage relative to the other intervals of each sample. Los percentiles are calculated separately in each sample, on their intervals; not comparan samples between sí for to same interval.

Con minimum percentile 1 and maximum 99, the acceptable range of each sample va of P1 to P99. Un interval can ser extreme per below or per arriba. Por example, if remains outside of ese range in 95 of 100 samples and the percentage is set to 90 %, GATK lo excludes; if remains outside in exactly 90, not lo excludes per this criterio.

Esquema conceptual: counts low extreme < P1 · range acceptable P1–P99 · counts high extreme > P99. Los percentiles describen the distribution of intervals within of to sample; the threshold of samples decide if the patrón is suficientemente consistente between the cohort.
