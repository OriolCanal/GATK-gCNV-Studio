Median normalized coverage ⓘ

Compara the coverage of the interval with the median of each sample. 1 = igual to esa reference; 0,5 = aproximadamente the mitad; 2 = aproximadamente the doble. Un value low or high indicates less or greater coverage relativa, not to llamada of number of copias per sí sola.

Coverage variability · Robust CV (%) ⓘ

Resume cuánto difiere the coverage normalized between samples of the interval. Cerca of 0 % = samples consistentes; cuanto greater the percentage, greater the dispersion. Una variability high can señalar to region difficult or heterogénea; there are not to threshold universal.

Depth mean vs MAD

Cada point represents an interval. Eje X: depth mean raw per base; eje Y: MAD of the coverage normalized between samples.

SituaciónInterpretación
Poca depth + MAD highRegión técnicamente difficult.
Poca depth + MAD lowRegión stable, pero of low coverage.
Mucha depth + MAD highPosible bias systematic or heterogeneidad.
Mucha depth + MAD lowRegión potencialmente robusta.
Select to point or to fila for inspect the interval.
